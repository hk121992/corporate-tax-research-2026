3. Methodology Notes

ComponentTypeMethodSource
Corporate income taxTaxes Borne — actual/est. ATO transparency data where matched; annual report estimate otherwise ATO Corporate Tax Transparency Report 2023–24
Employer payroll taxTaxes Borne — estimated max(0, state_wages − state_threshold) × state_rate State Revenue Offices — payrolltax.gov.au
Employee income tax (PAYG)Taxes Collected — estimated 5-quintile salary distribution per industry. E[tax(salary)] = Σ 0.2 × tax(salary_q) ABS Employee Earnings, Hours and Leave Survey (EEH)
Medicare levyTaxes Collected — estimated 2% of gross salary across quintile distribution ATO income tax rates
GST from employee spendingTaxes Collected — estimated after_tax × 75% consumption × GST coverage (38–47%) × (10/110) ABS Household Expenditure Survey 2021–22
Land tax (optional)Taxes Borne — rough estimate employees × homeownership_rate × median_land_value × effective_rate. Disabled by default. REIA, state revenue offices
Resource paymentsNOT a tax Royalties + PRRT paid to government as resource owner. Separate from tax totals. Company annual reports
